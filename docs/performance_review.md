# Performance evidence

The earlier review's implementation descriptions, bottlenecks and next-work
list are historical. The [preserved report](history/performance-review-pre-cleanup.md)
retains its measurements and workload descriptions without relabelling them
as current evidence.

For new work use:

- [Project performance objectives](../manual/project-goals.md#g11-performance-objectives).
- [V4 work entry point](archive_v4_plan.md) to locate the branch's evaluation
  contract, workload definitions and retained evidence.
- [Benchmark history](benchmark_history.md) and dated reports for prior findings.

Record the tested revision, protection mode, workload, cache state, measurement
method and limitations. A historical microbenchmark does not qualify a release.
